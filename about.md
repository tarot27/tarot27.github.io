## About the Summer School

TAROT (Training And Research On Testing) is a network bringing together students, faculty members, researchers and practitioners working in software and communication systems testing.

The TAROT Summer School provides opportunities to learn from experts, discuss ongoing research and connect with the wider testing community.

The 2027 edition takes place **7–10 June 2027** at **Aalto University, Espoo, Finland**. The detailed theme and session programme will be announced later.
