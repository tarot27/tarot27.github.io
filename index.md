## Welcome to TAROT 2027

TAROT (Training And Research On Testing) brings together students, academics, researchers and practitioners interested in software and communication systems testing.

The **TAROT Summer School 2027** will take place from **7 to 10 June 2027** at **Aalto University, Espoo, Finland**. The detailed session programme and lecturer announcements will follow.

### Programme at a glance

| Date | Preliminary programme |
| --- | --- |
| **Mon, 7 June** | Full-day Summer School programme |
| **Tue, 8 June** | Half-day Summer School; half-day joining the Secure Demo Day with universities |
| **Wed, 9 June** | Half-day Summer School; tours and dinner |
| **Thu, 10 June** | Full-day Summer School programme |
